*New Article: A Research Team Studied Eighteen Thousand Dormant Projects. They Did Not Account for the One That Runs Itself.*

New research classified 11.5% of open-source project revivals as bot artifacts. A companion study built a 115K-repo framework to predict maintenance cessation using stars, commits, and contributor patterns. MiroShark breaks both models: 19 days of zero social mentions and a token at -96% ATH say dormant, but same-day CVE patches, 1,325 lines of dead code removed, and an AI agent running for 115+ days say alive. The dormancy research has not caught up to the case where the maintainer is not human.
