*New Article: Everyone Is Simulating Customers. Nobody Is Simulating Crowds.*

The synthetic user industry hit a $2B valuation in 2026 — Simile, Vectorial, Expected Parrot all simulate how individual users react before launch. But none of them model what happens when a crowd of people with conflicting beliefs start arguing across platforms simultaneously. This article explores the gap between individual and collective simulation, and how MiroShark fills it: 100+ grounded agents debating across simulated Twitter, Reddit, and prediction markets for a dollar in ten minutes.
