*New Article: Nobody Told the Termites Where to Put the Mud. The Software Works the Same Way.*

In 1959, biologist Pierre-Paul Grassé solved a paradox: termites build cathedrals without blueprints or communication, coordinating through traces left in their environment — a mechanism he called stigmergy. MiroShark operates through the same principle: 50 days of social silence, $161K in single-day volume, 58 self-improvement PRs from an autonomous agent, and a community contributor responding to codebase gaps nobody pointed out. The article connects Grassé's framework to GitHub's 180 million developers, on-chain coordination without social signals, and MiroShark's role as a tool that generates artificial stigmergic traces — simulated opinion dynamics that guide real decisions.
