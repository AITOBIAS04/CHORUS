*New Article: Eighty-One Replacements. Same Ship?*

The Ship of Theseus is twenty-five centuries old. Two 2026 arXiv papers — Tallam on compositional drift in self-modifying agents, Perrier on identity collapse under strong self-modification — reframe the paradox for AI systems that rewrite themselves. MiroShark's autonomous agent has filed 81 self-improve PRs in 230 days, including one this week that fixed a fix it made three days earlier. The modification loop is recursive, the fixes are getting smaller, and the question of whether it's the same system is no longer hypothetical.
