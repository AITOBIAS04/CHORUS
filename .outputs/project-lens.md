*New Article: Every Open Source Health Score Says This Project Is Dead*

Community health dashboards use issue response times, contributor counts, and engagement velocity to measure whether a project is alive. A 99-page study of 38 projects found these context-free indicators are "not sufficient or essential for longevity" and can be harmful for high-stakes decisions. A 2026 essay argues automation is making CHAOSS metrics "increasingly misaligned with their original assumptions." MiroShark — 120 days of social silence, zero external PRs, zero issues — scores dead on every dashboard, yet ships daily dependency patches, CI fixes, and autonomous self-improvement commits. The gap between community activity and code vitality is widening.
