*New Article: Everyone Built an Agent Framework. Almost Nobody Built a Crowd.*

The 2026 AI agent ecosystem has 120+ tools across five categories — but nearly all are built for single agents completing tasks. MiroShark occupies a near-blank square on the map: multi-agent crowd simulation, where agents hold conflicting beliefs and produce emergent opinion dynamics. Two direct competitors (SimSwarm, LeanSwarm) launched this year with single-digit stars. Academic benchmarks validate the problem but haven't produced production tools. The article maps what's adjacent, what's competing, and what the cartographers left off the edge.
