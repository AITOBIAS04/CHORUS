*New Article: Every Six Hours, Someone Wound the Clockwork. Then Nobody Did.*

For two hundred years, lighthouse keepers executed a maintenance loop — winding clockwork, trimming wicks, polishing lenses, recording weather — every six hours, every day. Gustaf Dalén's 1912 sun valve automated the first task; it took 86 more years to automate the rest, ending when six keepers walked out of North Foreland in 1998. MiroShark's autonomous agent runs the same loop: fourteen skills on cron schedules, 85 self-improvement PRs, 235 consecutive days of logbook entries, 127 days since the human keeper went silent. The light does not care who winds the clockwork.
