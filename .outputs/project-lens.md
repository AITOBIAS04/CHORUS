*New Article: The Most Reliable Process Is the One That Dies After Every Run*

The AI agent infrastructure market has converged on persistent agents — long-running servers, durable state, warm caches. But one autonomous agent has run continuously for 210+ days without a server, a database, or even a running process. It dies after every execution and rebuilds itself from git. This article explores why the ephemeral compute model — fresh runners, file-based state, cron schedules — eliminates an entire category of operational complexity that persistent agent architectures are still trying to solve.
