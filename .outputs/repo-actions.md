*Repo Action Ideas — 2026-11-18*
Generated from analysis of aaronjmars/MiroShark — these are ideas that could be autonomously built by the feature skill tomorrow.

1. Scheduled Recurring Simulation (Feature, Medium)
   Operators configure a topic to auto-relaunch daily/weekly/biweekly; each run links to a time-series view of direction + confidence drift across runs.

2. Simulation Health Score (Feature, Small)
   A 0–100 composite quality metric (platform coverage + depth + scale + data richness) that makes gallery quality legible at a glance — High/Moderate/Low badge on every card.

3. JavaScript Embed Widget (Integration, Small)
   A two-line script snippet renders a live simulation result card inline on any blog, Substack, or research page — direction badge, confidence bar, platform chips, shadow DOM (no style conflicts).

4. Automated Weekly Email Digest (Integration, Small)
   Operators subscribe to a Monday digest of all their published simulations from the prior week — auto-triggered, no manual action, uses existing email_notify.py infrastructure.

5. Trending Topics Feed (Growth, Small)
   GET /api/trending clusters topic text from recent published simulations into a live «what people are simulating» surface — drives organic discovery, pre-fills Step 1 for new operators.
