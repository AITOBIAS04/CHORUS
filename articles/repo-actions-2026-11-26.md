### 5. Gallery Trending Feed

**Type:** Feature
**Effort:** Small (hours)
**Impact:** The MiroShark gallery shows published simulations in reverse-chronological order. There's no "trending" or "hot" sort: a simulation published 3 weeks ago that's receiving views and shares today appears below dozens of newer but less-engaged simulations. `backend/app/services/feed.py` and `backend/app/services/gallery_filters.py` exist — the filter infrastructure is in place. The clone and embed counts in the heat formula reward simulations that drive downstream activity — aligning the trending signal with what actually creates value.

**How:**
1. Create `backend/app/services/trending_service.py` (~120 LoC, pure stdlib). `compute_heat_score(sim_id: str, signal_weights: dict = None) -> float`: default weights `{views_24h: 2.0, views_7d: 0.5, clone_count: 5.0, embed_count: 3.0}`. Return `sum(weight * signal for weight, signal in zip(weights, signals))`. `build_trending_feed(limit: int = 10) -> list`: scan all published completed sims, compute heat scores, sort descending, return top `limit` entries as `{sim_id, topic, direction, confidence_pct, heat_score, created_at}`.
